CipherIQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Developer: Creative Nerd · Contact: 20thmarch2025@gmail.com

CipherIQ is a number code-breaking puzzle game for Android ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. "We" means Creative Nerd, the developer of the app.

1. The short version

  • You can play the whole game without an account. Your progress then stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google, we back up your game progress to the cloud so you can restore it.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase) to improve the game.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • You can delete your account and cloud data at any time (see section 7).

3. Ads and your choices

CipherIQ shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to get a hint). Ads are served by Google AdMob and may use your advertising ID. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy options when it is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). You can remove banner ads with an in-app purchase.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app, and only for the purposes above:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Billing). See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Children

CipherIQ is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep information

Game data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or use Start fresh. Cloud save data stays until you delete your account. Analytics and crash data are kept according to Google's standard retention settings for Firebase. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to deliver and restore your purchases.

7. Your choices and rights, including deletion

  • Delete your account and cloud data: in the app go to Settings > Delete account and cloud data. You can also request deletion on our account deletion page without reinstalling the app.
  • Play without an account: simply do not sign in.
  • Ads: manage consent in Settings > Privacy options (where shown) and in your Android ad settings.
  • Notifications: switch reminders off in the app or in Android system settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to use these rights.

8. Security and international transfers

Data sent between the app and our service providers is encrypted in transit. Google processes data on servers in several countries, so your information may be transferred outside your country.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
