Delete your CipherIQ account and data

App: CipherIQ · Developer: Creative Nerd

You can delete your CipherIQ account and the game data we store in the cloud at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open CipherIQ and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and cloud data and confirm.

This deletes your cloud save and your sign-in account. Game progress already on your phone stays there until you use Start fresh or uninstall the app.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Send an email to 20thmarch2025@gmail.com with the subject CipherIQ account deletion request, from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in. We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your cloud save: statistics, coins, level, achievements, streaks and items stored under your account.
  • Your sign-in account (name, email address and account ID held by Firebase Authentication).

What may be kept

  • Purchase records held by Google Play (they belong to your Google account and are managed by Google).
  • Aggregated or anonymous analytics and crash data that cannot identify you, kept under Google's standard Firebase retention settings.

See our Privacy Policy for more detail.
